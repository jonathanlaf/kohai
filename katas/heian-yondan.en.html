Heian Yondan 平安四段

Peaceful mind, fourth level

Heian Yondan, the fourth Heian kata, has 27 moves, with two Kiai: on the 13th and the 25th move. It is the longest of the Heian kata. It is required for the grading from green belt to blue belt. Its embusen is almost shaped like an “I.”

Also read, common to every kata: The spirit of the kata and Common mistakes.

Breaking down the kata 型, step by step

Heian Yondan is the kata of leg techniques: two side kicks (yoko geri keage), three front kicks (mae geri) and a knee strike (hiza geri). It also introduces the back-of-hand block (haishu uke), the X-block (juji uke), the wedge block (kakiwake uke), the elbow strike into the palm and the crossed-feet stance (Kosa Dachi). The rhythm alternates slow moves (1, 2, 14 and 18) and fast ones. You must finish the kata exactly at the starting point.

Embusen 演武線 (line of movement)

The kata follows an almost “I”-shaped pattern, with a “V” at the far end. Moves 1 and 2 are at the starting point, to the left then to the right. You move forward on the centre line (3 and 4), do the kicks and elbow strikes to the left then to the right (5 to 10), then go forward to the end of the line (11 to 13, Kiai on the 13th). The two rear diagonals start from there, first to the left (14 to 17), then to the right (18 to 21). You then come back on the centre line toward the starting point (22 to 25, Kiai on the 25th). The turn of move 26 puts the right foot down just behind the starting point, and move 27 brings you back exactly to the starting point.

Heian Yondan 平安四段 kata tutorial

MoveTechniqueStanceDirection
Rei 礼 (bow)Standing bow (Ritsurei)Musubi Dachi (attention stance)Heels together, feet open at 45°, hands flat along the body. Bow by tilting the upper body forward, eyes on the floor about two metres ahead. The sensei announces the kata: answer “Heian Yondan!” in a strong voice.
Yoi 用意 (start)Ready positionHeiko Dachi (parallel stance)From Musubi Dachi, open the left foot then the right foot to shoulder width, closed fists lowered naturally in front of the body. Eyes straight ahead, at the starting point of the embusen.
1. Ichi 一 (one)Left back-of-hand block (haishu uke), jodan, and right high guard (jodan kamae), open handsKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Turn your head to the left and step the left foot 90° to the left. Slow move (about three seconds).
2. Ni 二 (two)Right back-of-hand block (haishu uke), jodan, and left high guard (jodan kamae), open handsKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Turn your head to the right and pivot both feet 180° to the right. Quick pivot, then a slow move.
3. San 三 (three)Downward X-block (gedan juji uke), closed fistsZenkutsu Dachi (front stance)Turn to the front and step the left foot forward on the centre line.
4. Shi 四 (four)Right double block (morote uke), chudan: the right forearm blocks, the left fist supports the right elbowKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Step the right foot forward on the centre line.
5. Go 五 (five)Fists at the right hip (ryoken koshi gamae), left fist resting on the rightAshi Dachi (on one leg)Turn your head to the left and bring the left foot up by the right knee, standing on the right leg.
6. Roku 六 (six)Left side kick (yoko geri keage) and left back-fist strike (uraken uchi), jodan, at the same timeAshi Dachi (on one leg)To the left, on the right leg.
7. Shichi 七 (seven)Right elbow strike (mae empi uchi) into the left palm, chudanZenkutsu Dachi (front stance)Put the left foot down to the left (90° from the centre line) and strike with the right elbow into the left palm.
8. Hachi 八 (eight)Fists at the left hip (ryoken koshi gamae), right fist resting on the leftAshi Dachi (on one leg)Turn your head to the right, draw the left foot back slightly and bring the right foot up by the left knee, standing on the left leg.
9. Ku 九 (nine)Right side kick (yoko geri keage) and right back-fist strike (uraken uchi), jodan, at the same timeAshi Dachi (on one leg)To the right, on the left leg.
10. Ju 十 (ten)Left elbow strike (mae empi uchi) into the right palm, chudanZenkutsu Dachi (front stance)Put the right foot down to the right (90° from the centre line) and strike with the left elbow into the right palm.
11. Ju Ichi 十一 (eleven)Left knife-hand downward sweep (shuto gedan barai), then left knife-hand high block (jodan uke) and right circular knife-hand strike (shuto uchi), jodanZenkutsu Dachi (front stance)Do the sweep in place, then pivot on both feet to face the front, left foot forward, blocking and striking at the same time.
12. Ju Ni 十二 (twelve)Right front kick (mae geri keage), jodanAshi Dachi (on one leg)Without moving the arms, kick forward.
13. Ju San 十三 (thirteen)Left pressing block (osae uke), then right vertical back-fist strike (tate uraken uchi), jodan, and 1st KiaiKosa Dachi (crossed feet)Put the right foot down far forward: the left foot crosses behind the right.
14. Ju Shi 十四 (fourteen)Double wedge block (kakiwake uke), chudanKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Pivot on the right foot and move the left foot toward the rear-left diagonal. Slow move.
15. Ju Go 十五 (fifteen)Right front kick (mae geri keage), chudanAshi Dachi (on one leg)Along the diagonal, forward.
16. Ju Roku 十六 (sixteen)Right stepping punch (oi zuki), chudanZenkutsu Dachi (front stance)Put the right foot down in front after the kick.
17. Ju Shichi 十七 (seventeen)Left reverse punch (gyaku zuki), chudanZenkutsu Dachi (front stance)In place, with a sharp hip rotation.
18. Ju Hachi 十八 (eighteen)Double wedge block (kakiwake uke), chudanKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Pivot on the left foot (the back foot) and move the right foot toward the rear-right diagonal. Slow move.
19. Ju Ku 十九 (nineteen)Left front kick (mae geri keage), chudanAshi Dachi (on one leg)Along the diagonal, forward.
20. Ni Ju 二十 (twenty)Left stepping punch (oi zuki), chudanZenkutsu Dachi (front stance)Put the left foot down in front after the kick.
21. Ni Ju Ichi 二十一 (twenty-one)Right reverse punch (gyaku zuki), chudanZenkutsu Dachi (front stance)In place, with a sharp hip rotation.
22. Ni Ju Ni 二十二 (twenty-two)Left double block (morote uke), chudan: the left forearm blocks, the right fist supports the left elbowKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Turn your head to the left and pivot 45° on the right foot to face the rear, toward the starting point, stepping the left foot forward.
23. Ni Ju San 二十三 (twenty-three)Right double block (morote uke), chudanKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Step the right foot forward on the centre line.
24. Ni Ju Shi 二十四 (twenty-four)Left double block (morote uke), chudanKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Step the left foot forward on the centre line.
25. Ni Ju Go 二十五 (twenty-five)Two-handed head grab (morote kubi osae) and right knee strike (hiza geri), and 2nd KiaiZenkutsu Dachi (front stance), then Ashi Dachi (on one leg)Shift into Zenkutsu Dachi without moving the feet, reaching both hands forward at face height, then pull them down as you drive the right knee up.
26. Ni Ju Roku 二十六 (twenty-six)Left knife-hand block (shuto uke), chudanKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Pivot 180° on the left foot and put the right foot down behind you: you face the front, just behind the starting point.
27. Ni Ju Shichi 二十七 (twenty-seven)Right knife-hand block (shuto uke), chudanKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Step the right foot forward: you are at the starting point.
Yame 止め (end)Back to the ready positionShizentai (natural stance)Bring the front (right) foot back to the ready position, facing the front, at the starting point, closed fists in front of the body.
Rei 礼 (bow)Standing bow (Ritsurei)Musubi Dachi (attention stance)From Yoi Dachi, bring the feet together in Musubi Dachi. Bow by tilting the upper body forward, eyes on the floor about two metres ahead.
