Heian Godan 平安五段

Peaceful mind, fifth level

Heian Godan, the fifth and last Heian kata, has 23 moves, with two Kiai: on the 12th and the 19th move. It is required for the grading from blue belt to brown belt. Its embusen is shaped like a “T.”

Also read, common to every kata: The spirit of the kata and Common mistakes.

Breaking down the kata 型, step by step

Heian Godan is the kata of variety: it links many new techniques and stances, such as gyaku zuki in Kokutsu Dachi, upward and downward X-blocks (juji uke), the crescent kick (mikazuki geri), the L-shaped stance (Renoji Dachi), the manji uke double block and, above all, a jump that dodges an attack to the legs and lands in Kosa Dachi. The rhythm alternates strong, fast moments and slower, more focused ones (moves 3, 6, 14 and 22). It is often the hardest of the five Heian: several techniques need good stability and precise weight shifts.

Embusen 演武線 (line of movement)

The kata follows a “T”-shaped pattern. Moves 1 to 6 are on the starting line, to the left then to the right. You go forward on the centre line (7 to 12, Kiai on the 12th), then come back toward the start in Kiba Dachi (13 to 18) and with a jump (19, Kiai). Moves 20 to 23 are on the starting line, on the right side: 20 and 23 face the right, 21 faces the left.

Zenkutsu Dachi 前屈立ち (front stance) Kokutsu Dachi 後屈立ち (back stance) Kiba Dachi 騎馬立ち (horse stance) Several moves at the same spot Kiai 気合

Rei 礼 (bow)Standing bow (Ritsurei)Musubi Dachi (attention stance)Heels together, feet open at 45°, hands flat along the body. Bow by tilting the upper body forward, eyes on the floor about two metres ahead. The sensei announces the kata: answer “Heian Godan!” in a strong voice.
Yoi 用意 (start)Ready positionHeiko Dachi (parallel stance)From Musubi Dachi, open the left foot then the right foot to shoulder width, closed fists lowered naturally in front of the body. Eyes straight ahead, at the starting point of the embusen.

13. Ju San 十三 (thirteen)Right downward sweep (gedan barai)Kiba Dachi (horse stance)Pivot 180° on the left foot to the left, raise the right knee and stamp the right foot down into Kiba Dachi, looking toward the starting point.
14. Ju Shi 十四 (fourteen)Left back-of-hand block (haishu uke), chudan, slowlyKiba Dachi (horse stance)Turn your head to the other side and slowly extend the open left hand at shoulder height.
15. Ju Go 十五 (fifteen)Right crescent kick (mikazuki geri) into the left palmAshi Dachi (on one leg)Pivot on the left foot and strike the left palm with the sole of the right foot.
16. Ju Roku 十六 (sixteen)Right elbow strike (mae empi uchi) into the left palm, chudanKiba Dachi (horse stance)Put the right foot down in Kiba Dachi and strike with the right elbow into the left palm.
17. Ju Shichi 十七 (seventeen)Right double block (morote uke), chudan, to the right sideKosa Dachi (crossed feet)Slide the left foot behind the right foot, heel raised.
18. Ju Hachi 十八 (eighteen)Right rising punch (tsukiage), jodan, left fist near the right elbowRenoji Dachi (L-shaped stance)Draw the left foot back slightly, straightening the right leg, and turn your head and hips to the left as the fists rise.
19. Ju Ku 十九 (nineteen)Jump, then downward X-block (gedan juji uke), and 2nd KiaiKosa Dachi (crossed feet)Pivot to the left on the left foot and jump high with a half turn, fists at the hips and knees drawn up; land in a low Kosa Dachi, upper body upright.
20. Ni Ju 二十 (twenty)Right double block (morote uke), chudanZenkutsu Dachi (front stance)Step the right foot 90° to the right.

Ni Ju San 二十三 (twenty-three)Right sweeping block (nagashi uke), jodan, and left knife-hand downward thrust (shuto gedan uchikomi), then double block (manji uke): left uchi uke high and behind, right gedan baraiZenkutsu Dachi (front stance), then Kokutsu Dachi (back stance)Step the right foot to the right; then draw the right foot back slightly into Kokutsu Dachi for the manji uke.

Rei 礼 (bow)Standing bow (Ritsurei)Musubi Dachi (attention stance)From Yoi Dachi, bring the feet together in Musubi Dachi. Bow by tilting the upper body forward, eyes on the floor about two metres ahead.
