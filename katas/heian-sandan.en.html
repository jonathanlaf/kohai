Heian Sandan 平安三段

Peaceful mind, third level

Heian Sandan, the third Heian kata, has 20 moves, with two Kiai: on the 10th and the 20th move. It is required for the grading from orange belt to green belt. Its embusen is shaped like a “T.”

Also read, common to every kata: The spirit of the kata and Common mistakes.

Breaking down the kata 型, step by step

Almost half of the kata is done in Kiba Dachi (horse stance), a new stance compared with Heian Nidan. Heian Sandan also introduces double blocks on two levels (uchi uke and gedan barai at the same time) with the feet together in Heisoku Dachi, elbow techniques (empi), the foot stamp (fumikomi) and a defence against a grab from behind at the end of the kata. The rhythm changes: the four steps forward are linked, move 11 is done slowly, and the way back is clearly marked.

Embusen 演武線 (line of movement)

The kata follows a “T”-shaped pattern. Moves 1 to 6 are on the starting line, to the left then to the right. You then go forward on the centre line (7 to 10, Kiai on the 10th), turn around slowly (11), then come back toward the start in Kiba Dachi (12 to 17), changing sides with each foot stamp. Move 18 brings you back to the starting point, where the last two moves are done (19 and 20, Kiai on the 20th), facing the front. Finally, Yame brings you back to the starting point.

Zenkutsu Dachi 前屈立ち (front stance) Kokutsu Dachi 後屈立ち (back stance) Kiba Dachi 騎馬立ち (horse stance) Kiai 気合 Yame 止め (back to the start)

Heian Sandan 平安三段 kata tutorial

MoveTechniqueStanceDirection
Rei 礼 (bow)Standing bow (Ritsurei)Musubi Dachi (attention stance)Heels together, feet open at 45°, hands flat along the body. Bow by tilting the upper body forward, eyes on the floor about two metres ahead. The sensei announces the kata: answer “Heian Sandan!” in a strong voice.
Yoi 用意 (start)Ready positionHeiko Dachi (parallel stance)From Musubi Dachi, open the left foot then the right foot to shoulder width, closed fists lowered naturally in front of the body. Eyes straight ahead, at the starting point of the embusen.
1. Ichi 一 (one)Left inside-out block (uchi uke), chudanKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Turn your head to the left and step the left foot 90° to the left.
2. Ni 二 (two)Right uchi uke, chudan, and left downward sweep (gedan barai), at the same timeHeisoku Dachi (feet together)Bring the right foot against the left foot, knees slightly bent. The arms cross in front of the body before the double block.
3. San 三 (three)Left uchi uke, chudan, and right gedan barai, at the same timeHeisoku Dachi (feet together)In place: switch arms in one motion, linked with move 2.
4. Shi 四 (four)Right uchi uke, chudanKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Turn your head to the right, pivot on the left foot and step the right foot to the right (half turn).
5. Go 五 (five)Left uchi uke, chudan, and right gedan barai, at the same timeHeisoku Dachi (feet together)Bring the left foot against the right foot.
6. Roku 六 (six)Right uchi uke, chudan, and left gedan barai, at the same timeHeisoku Dachi (feet together)In place: switch arms, linked with move 5.
7. Shichi 七 (seven)Left double block (morote uke), chudan: the left forearm blocks, the right fist supports the left elbowKokutsu Dachi (back stance)Turn your head to the front, pivot on the right foot and step the left foot onto the centre line.
8. Hachi 八 (eight)Right spear hand (tate shihon nukite), chudan, and left pressing block (osae uke)Zenkutsu Dachi (front stance)Step the right foot forward on the centre line.
9. Ku 九 (nine)Left circular hammer-fist strike (tettsui uchi), chudanKiba Dachi (horse stance)Turn the right hand down, as if freeing it from a grab, pivot 180° on the right foot to the left and move the left foot further along the centre line.
10. Ju 十 (ten)Right stepping punch (oi zuki), chudan, and 1st KiaiZenkutsu Dachi (front stance)Step the right foot forward on the centre line.
11. Ju Ichi 十一 (eleven)Fists on the hips (ryoken koshi kamae)Heisoku Dachi (feet together)Slowly: pivot on the right foot and bring the left foot against the right foot in a half turn, facing the starting point.
12. Ju Ni 十二 (twelve)Right foot stamp (fumikomi), gedan, and right elbow block (empi uke), chudanKiba Dachi (horse stance)Raise the right knee high, then stamp the right foot down, moving along the centre line toward the starting point.
13. Ju San 十三 (thirteen)Right vertical back-fist strike (tate uraken uchi), jodanKiba Dachi (horse stance)In place, then bring the right fist back to the hip.
14. Ju Shi 十四 (fourteen)Left foot stamp (fumikomi), gedan, and left elbow block (empi uke), chudanKiba Dachi (horse stance)Pivot 180° on the right foot, raise the left knee and stamp the left foot down, moving along the centre line.
15. Ju Go 十五 (fifteen)Left vertical back-fist strike (tate uraken uchi), jodanKiba Dachi (horse stance)In place, then bring the left fist back to the hip.
16. Ju Roku 十六 (sixteen)Right foot stamp (fumikomi), gedan, and right elbow block (empi uke), chudanKiba Dachi (horse stance)Pivot 180° on the left foot, raise the right knee and stamp the right foot down, moving along the centre line.
17. Ju Shichi 十七 (seventeen)Right vertical back-fist strike (tate uraken uchi), jodanKiba Dachi (horse stance)In place, then bring the right fist back to the hip.
18. Ju Hachi 十八 (eighteen)Left vertical knife-hand block (tate shuto uke), slow, then left stepping punch (oi zuki), chudanKiba Dachi (horse stance), then Zenkutsu Dachi (front stance)Do the tate shuto uke in place, slowly, breathing in. Then step the left foot forward on the centre line and punch with the left fist.
19. Ju Ku 十九 (nineteen)Right vertical punch over the left shoulder (tate zuki), jodan, and left back elbow strike (ushiro empi uchi), chudanKiba Dachi (horse stance)Bring the right foot against the left foot, then pivot 180° on the right foot: you are in Kiba Dachi, facing the front, at the starting point. The elbow strikes behind you.
20. Ni Ju 二十 (twenty)Left vertical punch over the right shoulder (tate zuki), jodan, and right back elbow strike (ushiro empi uchi), chudan, and 2nd KiaiKiba Dachi (horse stance)Slide to the right (yori ashi), staying in Kiba Dachi.
Yame 止め (end)Back to the ready positionShizentai (natural stance)Bring the feet back to the ready position, facing the front, at the starting point, closed fists in front of the body.
Rei 礼 (bow)Standing bow (Ritsurei)Musubi Dachi (attention stance)From Yoi Dachi, bring the feet together in Musubi Dachi. Bow by tilting the upper body forward, eyes on the floor about two metres ahead.
