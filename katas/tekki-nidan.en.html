Tekki Nidan 鉄騎二段

Iron horseman, second level

Tekki Nidan, the second of the three Tekki kata and the shortest, has 24 moves, with two Kiai: on the 16th and the 24th move. It is required for the black belt grading, 2nd dan. Like Tekki Shodan, it is done on a single line, sideways.

Also read, common to every kata: The spirit of the kata and Common mistakes.

Breaking down the kata 型, step by step

Almost the whole kata is done in Kiba Dachi, except for the crossing steps (Kosa Dachi) and one moment with the feet together (Heisoku Dachi, move 5). It opens with forearm blocks and feints, thumbs spread (1 to 8), then introduces the supported block (soete uchi uke), the elbow strike with a foot stamp and the grasping block (tsukami uke), done slowly. Moves 13 to 16 and 21 to 24 are the same as in Tekki Shodan. The second half (17 to 24) repeats the first on the other side.

Embusen 演武線 (floor pattern)

The kata follows a single straight line. You set off to the right with two crossing steps (1 to 4), bring the feet together (5), then come back to the starting point (6 to 8), where moves 9 to 13 are done. A crossing step takes you back to the right (14 to 21, Kiai on the 16th), and a last crossing step returns you to the starting point (22 to 24, Kiai on the 24th).
