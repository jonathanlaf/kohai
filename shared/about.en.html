A Kōhai’s notes 後輩

My name is Jonathan Lafleur and I am a Kōhai 後輩 (student) at the dojo where I practise Shotokan karate. I am just starting out, so errors may slip into the documents on this site.

The content is adapted from what I learn at my dojo and from public sources. This site is not an official document: it is not produced, reviewed or endorsed by my dojo, and is not affiliated with any dojo or organization. The dojo 道場, the sensei 先生 and the senpai 先輩 are in no way responsible for the information shared here.

Read these pages as a student’s notes: they may differ from what is taught in your dojo. When in doubt, your sensei’s teaching comes first.

Why I share my notes

I believe information is worth sharing with as many people as possible. If these notes help me review, they can also help other students prepare for a class or a grading. The site is free to use and each set of notes prints with its table of contents.

Found an error? Tell me

Something inaccurate, unclear or awkwardly translated? Write to me at me@jonathanlafleur.ca and mention the page, or tell me about it at the dojo. I will fix any error as quickly as I can.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

Your theme, paper size and language choices are saved in your browser (local storage). They are never sent anywhere and are erased with this site’s data in your browser.

Videos come from YouTube (Google) and only load when you click to play them. From that moment, Google’s privacy policy applies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps technical logs (including IP addresses) for security purposes, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Sources and credits

Texts drawn from English-language sources have been adapted and modified for these notes. Thanks to their authors.
