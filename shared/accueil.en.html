Kohai 後輩

My personal study notes on Shotokan karate katas.

In the dojo, the kohai is the student who learns from their elders, the senpai. These notes keep them company between classes: for each kata, its spirit, its embusen, every move illustrated and explained, a video, the common mistakes and the Japanese vocabulary. Each set of notes prints with its own table of contents. They complement the sensei’s teaching without replacing it. Who am I and why this site?
