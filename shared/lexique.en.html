Karateka’s vocabulary 空手家

The basic words you need to understand the sensei’s instructions. After another word, “tsuki” becomes “zuki,” “keri” becomes “geri” and “harai” becomes “barai” (Oi Zuki, Mae Geri, Gedan Barai): it is the same word, pronounced more softly.

Pronunciation is written for English speakers; the stressed syllable is not marked, since Japanese gives every syllable about the same weight. In Japanese, “a” is said “ah,” “e” is said “eh,” “i” is said “ee,” “u” is said “oo,” and “g” is always hard, as in “go.” A final “u” is often barely pronounced: Osu is said “oss.”

Technique names read like compound words: Yoko Geri Keage is “side” + “kick” + “snapping upward”. The sections below give these pieces one by one.
