Kohai’s Games 遊び

Study by playing: small games to remember what you learn at the dojo, between classes. Your best scores stay in your browser.

  • 言葉 Learn the language Cards for each vocabulary section: find the meaning of each Japanese word among three answers.
  • 型 Learn the katas Put the moves of a kata back in order, five at a time, by dragging them.

Learn the language 言葉

Pick a vocabulary section. Each card shows a Japanese word: find its meaning among three answers. Up to ten random cards per game.

Learn the katas 型

Pick a kata. Its moves come five at a time, shuffled: drag them (or use the arrows) to put them back in order, then check. A game goes through the whole kata.
