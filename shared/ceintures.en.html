Belts and ranks (Obi) 帯

The colour of the belt (obi, 帯) shows the rank. There are two series: kyu 級, the student ranks in coloured belts, and dan 段, the black-belt ranks.

The stripes on the brown belt count down the remaining kyu; the bars on the black belt count the dan. The colours, and the kyu number of the coloured belts, change from one association to another (some add purple or half-belts): this site follows the most common order, but your dojo has the last word.

Kyu and dan

  • Kyu 級, “class”: counted down. The lower the number, the further along you are, up to 1st kyu, the last one before black belt.
  • Dan 段, “degree, step”: counted up, from 1st dan (shodan, 初段, “first degree”) to 10th dan.
  • The black belt is not an end: shodan means you finally know the basics. High dan ranks recognize years of practice and teaching (see titles).
  • Shodan, Nidan, Sandan… are the same words as in the kata names: Heian Shodan is the “first level” of the Heian series.
